OUR FOUNDATION

The PratecktaSoft Story: Secure Growth

**PratecktaSoft** was founded in **2018** on a core principle: **protection** (`Prateckta`) must be seamlessly integrated into modern **software** (`Soft`). We saw a gap where businesses had to choose between speed and security. Our mission became to bridge that gap, delivering **bespoke, high-performance software** that is secure by design.

We've grown from a small consultancy to a full-service digital transformation agency, guided by our core values: **Transparency, Engineering Excellence, and Client Partnership.** Our success is a direct result of our focused approach on cloud-native and secure coding practices.
